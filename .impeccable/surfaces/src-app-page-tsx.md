# Badd Boys app shell and Kvöldið

Scope: the whole installable app (shell, four tabs, full-screen moments), led by the Kvöldið score pad. Mode: Operate.

Audience and job: the weekly group, courtside under bright hall lights, logging a final score between games in seconds; browsing stats afterwards. Constraints: light ground (dark and neon rejected), high contrast, large targets, Icelandic copy unchanged, only the name Badd Boys is binding.

Chosen direction: The court (chosen by the user from the decision round; the roll's club-pennant direction was declined). Memorable moment: the score pad as a top-down doubles court.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The hall is the interface. Kvöldið's score pad is a top-down doubles court where each player stands in a service box and the two scores face each other across the net. It refuses the dark arena with neon glows and the white rounded-card tracker.

OWN-WORLD: Cool light hall-floor ground; white panels as flat fields, no borders or glows. Court-mat green for the court, the side holding it and every win; white line tape as the only rules on green; shuttle-tube yellow fills for the primary action, runs and honors; challenger blue; an ink tab bar. Archivo throughout: extra-condensed heavy caps for numerals and labels, normal width for body, tabular figures. Tight 4px corners like taped lines.

STORY: A player glances at the bench phone, sees who holds the court and any run, taps four names into service boxes, sets the score at the net and logs it with one yellow button. The same palette carries leaderboard, pairs and records.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Light header with the condensed BADD BOYS wordmark, a court-line mark and the evening. The run strip when a pair is on a run. The green court full width: Lið A half above, the net band with the swap button, Lið B below; service boxes hold names, front zones hold minus, score and plus, back alleys hold side label, Setja 11 and 7–0. Player chips sit between the heading and the court so entry runs top to bottom (pick, score, log); the full-width yellow log button follows the court. Ink tab bar at the bottom.
